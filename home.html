I 06:10 Outdoor courts

First serve

The gates open before the traffic does. Ten courts outdoors, the surface still cold, and four people who agreed to this at eleven the night before. Off-peak runs until noon, which is the quietest bargain the club has.

II 17:40 Indoor hall

The hall

Inside, on sprung maple under twelve metres of clearance, the shuttle does what it can never do outdoors. No draught reaches these courts. Coached squads take one and two on weeknights; the rest is yours by the hour.

Floodlight

The rig comes on and the club changes shape. Four leagues run through the week, a scoreboard keeps everyone honest, and there is always a bench of people waiting on the winner. The last court goes dark at midnight.

Everything under one roof

Ten spaces, drawn to scale. The courts are the reason you come; the lounge, the terrace and the kitchen are the reason the two hours after your game take longer than the game.

Book a court

Six steps, about forty seconds. Availability is live, the price you see includes tax and your member discount, and nothing is charged until you confirm.

Ways to belong

Membership buys you two things a casual booking cannot: a longer window to grab the slot you actually want, and a reason to turn up on the days you had not planned to.
